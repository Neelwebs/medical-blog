Frequently Asked Questions

Which has a better career scope: BAMS or MBBS?

Both fields have great demand. MBBS excels in acute care and hospital surgeries, while BAMS is experiencing a massive global boom in preventive healthcare, wellness, and lifestyle management.

Is internship mandatory for both courses?

Yes, a 1-year compulsory rotating internship is mandatory for both BAMS and MBBS graduates before getting full medical practice registration.
