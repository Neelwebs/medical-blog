BAMS vs MBBS: The Ultimate Comparison

Choosing between Ayurveda and Modern Medicine after 12th.

Overview

Both medical fields offer incredible career opportunities, but they have completely different philosophies. MBBS focuses on modern allopathic medicine, pharmacology, and clinical surgeries, whereas BAMS focuses on ancient Indian Ayurvedic medicine, natural herbs, and root-cause healing.
