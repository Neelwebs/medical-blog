BAMS vs MBBS: The Ultimate Comparison

A comprehensive guide for students deciding between Ayurveda and Modern Medicine.

Introduction

Choosing between BAMS (Bachelor of Ayurvedic Medicine and Surgery) and MBBS (Bachelor of Medicine and Bachelor of Surgery) is a major milestone for medical aspirants. Both fields hold immense respect, but their philosophies and practice methods are distinct.

Quick Comparison Table

Feature BAMS (Ayurveda) MBBS (Allopathy)
Core Philosophy Natural balance, doshas, and root-cause healing Modern scientific medicine and targeted treatment
Course Duration 5.5 Years (including internship) 5.5 Years (including internship)
Treatment Style Herbal formulations, dietary guidelines, Panchakarma Pharmaceutical drugs, surgeries, emergency intervention

Frequently Asked Questions

Which has a better career scope: BAMS or MBBS?

Both fields have great demand. MBBS excels in acute care and hospital surgeries, while BAMS is experiencing a massive global boom in preventive healthcare, wellness, and lifestyle management.

Is internship mandatory for both courses?

Yes, a 1-year compulsory rotating internship is mandatory for both BAMS and MBBS graduates before getting full medical practice registration.
